Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Sri Renga Traders ("we", "us", "our"), our website rengaa.in, and the messages we send you about your orders, including on WhatsApp. By using our website or placing an order, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Delivery details: your street address, city, state, and PIN code.
  • Order details: the products you order, quantities, amounts, and order history.
  • Support conversations: messages you send us through live chat, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information, and items stored in your browser such as your cart and sign-in session.

We do not collect or store card or bank details. Orders are currently paid by Cash on Delivery.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack, deliver, and track your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates by SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your questions, replacement requests, and support needs.
  • To maintain your account and let you view your past orders.
  • To improve our products, website, and service, and to prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

WhatsApp Communication

When you place an order, we may send messages about that order to your mobile number through WhatsApp, using the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. These messages are about your orders and customer service. You can ask us to stop WhatsApp messages at any time by contacting us.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to serve you:

  • Delivery partners, to ship your order to you.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, live chat, and messaging (including Meta / WhatsApp).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our customers.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's storage to remember your cart and keep you signed in. Our live chat service may also set cookies to provide the chat feature. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

Data Security and Retention

We use secure (HTTPS) connections and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. We keep your information only as long as needed to fulfil your orders, provide support, and meet legal requirements.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you messages. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
