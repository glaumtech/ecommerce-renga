Overview

At Sri Renga Traders, customer satisfaction matters. If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or incorrect, you may request a replacement within 3 days of delivery.

3-Day Replacement Window

Replacement requests must be raised within 3 calendar days from the date of delivery. After this period, we may not be able to process a replacement.

Eligibility

  • The product arrived damaged, defective, or not as described.
  • You received the wrong item or an incomplete order.
  • The product is in its original condition and packaging where applicable.
  • You can provide your order number and supporting photos or details when requested.

How to Request a Replacement

  1. Contact our customer care team within 3 days of delivery.
  2. Share your order number, product name, and a brief description of the issue.
  3. Provide photos if the item is damaged or incorrect.
  4. Once approved, we will arrange a replacement according to product availability.

Items Not Eligible for Replacement

  • Requests made after the 3-day replacement window.
  • Products that have been used, opened, or altered beyond inspection, where hygiene or safety applies.
  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper handling after delivery.
